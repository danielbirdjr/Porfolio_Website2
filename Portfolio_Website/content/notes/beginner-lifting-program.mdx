Most people overcomplicate their first year of lifting. You don't need a six-day "bro split," a pre-workout stack, or a spreadsheet with twelve accessory movements. You need to show up three times a week, get stronger on a handful of compound lifts, and eat enough protein. That's it.

This is the program I send to every new coaching client who's starting out.

## The principles first

Before the program, internalize these. They matter more than the exact exercises.

1. **Progressive overload is the whole game.** Add a little weight or a rep whenever you can. If the bar never gets heavier, you never change.
2. **Consistency beats intensity.** Three solid workouts a week for a year beats six brutal workouts a week for three weeks.
3. **Technique is the rep.** A clean set of 8 builds more than a sloppy set of 12.

> If you remember nothing else: get stronger over time on a few big lifts, and be patient.

## The program

Run this three days a week — Monday, Wednesday, Friday works well. Alternate between Workout A and Workout B each session.

### Workout A

- Squat — 3 sets of 5
- Bench press — 3 sets of 5
- Barbell row — 3 sets of 8

### Workout B

- Deadlift — 1 set of 5
- Overhead press — 3 sets of 5
- Lat pulldown or pull-ups — 3 sets of 8

Warm up with lighter sets before your working weight, and rest 2–3 minutes between heavy sets.

## How to progress

Each time you complete all your reps with good form, add weight next session:

- Lower body lifts: add 5–10 lbs
- Upper body lifts: add 5 lbs

When you miss reps two sessions in a row, drop the weight by 10% and build back up. This is normal. It's not failure — it's the system working.

## What about everything else?

Abs, arms, cardio, mobility — they're fine to add, but they're seasoning, not the meal. Get the compound lifts moving first. Add a few sets of curls and calf raises at the end if you enjoy them.

Eat protein (roughly 0.7–1g per pound of bodyweight), sleep, and run this for six months before you even think about changing anything. Boring consistency is the cheat code.
